This is a blog, yes.

It's where I'll write down thought processes and inspirations — the half-formed ideas that I have that are immature and naive.

And I'll be honest: I usually prefer paper. Writing by hand is slow, but the slowness is the point. It retains a thought long enough to actually look at it. A notebook doesn't autocomplete, doesn't have tabs, and doesn't care whether anyone reads it.

But paper has a flaw: it stays on my desk. Nobody can argue with a notebook. So this is the deal I'm making with myself. The thinking still starts on paper, and the ideas that survive get typed up here (hopefully), where they can be read, questioned, and (hopefully) sharpened.

No promises about cadence. Posts will show up when a thought has earned one. If you're reading this: welcome, and feel free to push back.
